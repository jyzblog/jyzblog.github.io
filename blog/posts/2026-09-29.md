### **Studio Environment & Personal Space**
* **Historic Studio Location**: Andrew Huberman's studio is located in the former Los Angeles studio space of artist Jean-Michel Basquiat.
* **Space Purpose & Features**: Designed as a space to prepare for podcasts, work out, and house his dog, fish tanks, and red light technology.

### **Dietary Philosophy & Core Nutrition**
* **Consistent Omnivorous Diet**: Huberman has maintained the same unstaged eating pattern for 25 to 30 years, focusing on high-quality protein in every meal, fruits, vegetables, and healthy fats.
* **Fat Choices**: Prefers olive oil, a little butter, and Parmesan cheese while avoiding seed oils.
* **Carbohydrates & Starches**: Eats starches such as sourdough bread, rye bread, spelt bread (a mix of wheat, rye, and other grains), oatmeal, and rice, adjusting intake based on training volume.
* **Vegetables & History**: Known since college as "cucumber boy" for munching on whole cucumbers between classes for fiber; currently eats two to three Persian cucumbers daily.
* **Family Influences**: His Argentine father viewed grass-fed steak as a health food, while his mother’s passion for gardening instilled his love for fruits and vegetables.

### **Caffeine Strategy & Daily Routine**
* **Morning Hydration & Selection**: Always drinks water before caffeine, followed by organic, zero-sugar cold brew yerba mate (typically raspberry or mint flavor).
* **Caffeine Timing Rules**: 
  * If training first thing in the morning, drinks yerba mate immediately.
  * If not training immediately, delays caffeine intake by 60 to 90 minutes after waking to avoid an afternoon caffeine crash.
* **Caffeine Window**: Consumes caffeine until roughly 12:00 PM or 2:00 PM.

### **Meal Structure, Intermittent Fasting & Food Preferences**
* **Reflexive Intermittent Fasting**: Prefers to train in a fasted state in the morning, typically eating his first meal around 10:30 AM, 11:00 AM, or noon.
* **Fasting Exceptions**: If waking up exceptionally hungry, eats two scoops of whey protein with pistachios before training.
* **First Meal & Post-Workout Nutrition**: 
  * First meal often consists of a protein shake (whey protein, ice, and peanut butter for slower digestion) or lean steak with potatoes.
  * Consumes fruit post-training to restore blood sugar, along with starches to replenish glycogen after resistance workouts.
* **Primary Protein Sources**: Lean steak and chicken are go-to whole-food proteins; uses whey protein powders primarily when in a rush, noting that powder lacks essential micronutrients found in whole foods.
* **Special Foods & Habits**:
  * Consumes un-refrigerated fresh eggs provided by his friend and podcast filmer, Chris Ray.
  * Self-described "drive-by blueberry eater" who regularly grabs fistfuls of blueberries.
  * Indulges in a chocolate or ham-and-cheese croissant once every one to two weeks.

### **Foods to Avoid & Fermented Foods for Health**
* **Foods Avoided**: Recommends strictly avoiding fried foods and highly processed foods, noting they alter taste preferences, increase caloric intake, and dull the appreciation of natural whole foods.
* **Daily Fermented Foods**: Consumes low-sugar fermented foods daily to lower the "inflammatome" and systemic inflammation, citing a Stanford study by Justin Sonnenberg.
* **Fermented Choices & Voice Care**:
  * Eats Bulgarian yogurt (tangy and less creamy than Greek yogurt) and sauerkraut.
  * Drinks pomegranate vinegar in water as a low-sugar ferment preference over apple cider vinegar.
  * Drinks a couple of ounces of pure aloe daily—a tip learned from Dropkick Murphys singer Mike McColgan—to keep his voice smooth during heavy speaking.

### **Comfort Foods, Burger Order & Culinary Dislikes**
* **Comfort Food**: Oatmeal prepared with cinnamon, full-fat cream, and occasionally a small amount of maple syrup.
* **Specific Burger Order**: Double cheeseburger with cheese and pickles (no sweet pickles), served on a bun with a proper bun-to-beef ratio; strictly excludes lettuce and tomato from inside the burger, preferring a side salad instead.
* **Cooking & Disliked Foods**:
  * Excels at cooking steak both on an outdoor grill and indoors.
  * Strongly dislikes herring, sardines, tannic tea, and anything smoked (including smoked barbecue sauces and smoked salmon).
  * Enjoys spicy foods, hot sauces, and salsas.

### **Supplement Protocol & TRT/Peptides Philosophy**
* **Foundational Daily Supplements**:
  * **AG1**: Drinks two packets daily in water with lemon juice (taking it since 2012); currently uses AG1 Pro containing creatine and CoQ10.
  * **AGZ**: Taken before sleep.
  * **Vitamin D & K2**: Daily intake.
  * **Omega-3**: Takes Lovaza (prescribed high-purity omega-3) after a high-salmon diet previously elevated his blood mercury levels.
  * **Creatine**: Takes 10 grams daily due to his 220 lb body weight, taking a 10-day washout period every four to five months.
  * **Digestive Enzymes**: Taken with meals out of habit.
* **TRT Philosophy**: Advises men under 40 or 45 to optimize sleep and nutrition first; openly shares that he began a microdose TRT protocol at age 45 (15 mg every other day).
* **Peptides & GLP-1s**: Does not currently take peptides or GLP-1 medications (such as semaglutide, tirzepatide, or retatrutide), noting their potency and side effects.
* **Bedrock Health**: Emphasizes that sleep, nutritious food, physical training, sunlight, and social connection are far more critical than supplements.

### **Gym Setup, Workout Philosophy & Training Schedule**
* **Equipment & Logging**: Uses a mix of conventional and unconventional equipment, maintaining a detailed workout log book since age 16.
* **Training Frequency & Indirect Hitting**:
  * Direct training for each muscle group occurs once per week, with an indirecion 4 to 5 days later (e.g., direct leg training followed days later by sprinting or the Airdyne bike).
  * Exercises daily, reserving one rest day directly after leg day.
  * Weight workouts last approximately 1 hour following a 10-minute warmup.
  * High-intensity interval training (HIIT) sessions take approximately 8 minutes.
* **Progressive Overload & Deloads**: Focuses on adding weight or reps on at least one set per exercise weekly; takes one full week off every 14 to 16 weeks.

### **Key Exercise Equipment & Favorite Movements**
* **First-Gen Nautilus Pullover Machine**: Favorite back movement to isolate lats heavy without fatiguing the arms.
* **Grip Strength & Longevity**: Uses thick bar pull-ups, thick handle dips, and rotating thick dumbbells to build grip strength.
* **Heavy Belt Squats**: Favorite overall movement for the past 5 years because it allows maximum leg loading without putting weight on the shoulders or spine.

### **Exercise, Cognitive Performance & Health at 51**
* **Morning Cognitive Benefit**: Training before 9:00 AM leads to higher energy and mental sharpness throughout the day due to elevated post-exercise catecholamines (dopamine, norepinephrine, epinephrine). Training after 11:00 AM or noon can cause afternoon sleepiness.
* **Exercise vs. Intelligence**: Notes that while exercise boosts focus and maintains the brain, it does not inherently make a person smarter unless paired with active learning.
* **Current Health Status**: At age 51, reports feeling balanced and pain-free (resolving earlier lower back/hip pain from his 30s using an inversion table and advice from Jeff Cavalier and Bret Contreras).
* **Longevity Goals**: Aims to maintain all-around strength, endurance, and a lean frame into his 60s, 70s, and 80s, targeting a lifespan of up to 100 years.

### **Rapid-Fire Insights & Motivations**
* **Sleep vs. Meditation**: Chooses sleep.
* **Squats vs. Deadlifts**: Recommends doing both.
* **Dumbbells vs. Kettlebells**: Prefers dumbbells.
* **Cardio vs. Weights**: Prefers weights.
* **Big Legs vs. Big Arms**: Prioritizes legs as the foundation.
* **Primary Motivation**: Helping others.
